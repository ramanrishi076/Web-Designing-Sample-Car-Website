JAGUAR HERITAGE

The story behind the leaper.

A student-project timeline of major Jaguar milestones, presented as an animated visual journey.

1922

Swallow Sidecar Company

Founded in Blackpool, the company later evolved into the Jaguar marque.

1935

The Jaguar name

The Jaguar name was introduced with the SS Jaguar range.

1948

Jaguar XK120

The XK120 became an important post-war sports-car statement.

1961

E-type

The E-type became one of the most recognisable Jaguar sports cars.

1990

Ford era

Jaguar became part of Ford's Premier Automotive Group.

2008

Tata Motors

Tata Motors acquired Jaguar and Land Rover from Ford.

2024+

New creative era

Jaguar introduced a radical new design direction built around originality and the “Copy Nothing” philosophy.
